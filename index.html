How each stakeholder connects.

A closed-loop governance system linking students, canteen vendors, pharmacy inspectors, and campus authority.

ROLE 01

Student / Teacher

Submits evidence-backed complaints with photos, pre-orders daily meals to skip lines, and earns community trust points.

ROLE 02

Canteen Vendor

Receives real-time feedback, views AI demand forecast, rectifies flagged issues, and maintains high food hygiene standards.

ROLE 03

Pharmacy Inspector

Conducts scheduled and AI-triggered laboratory checks, files inspection audit logs, and validates high-risk reports.

ROLE 04

Campus Admin

Oversees live safety analytics, monitors vendor performance scorecards, and enforces administrative compliance actions.

Evidence-based reporting in action.

Submit structured complaints in under 30 seconds. Every report is cryptographically timestamped and processed by AI.

Why Canteen Watch is Different

A research-grounded comparison against existing food safety guidelines, supply chain traceability, and general inspection systems.

EXISTING APPROACH 01

Food Safety Guidelines

Main Contribution: Sets standard hygiene principles
Limitation in Campus: No digital way to submit/track student complaints
EXISTING APPROACH 02

Digital Traceability Systems

Main Contribution: Improves transparency & tracking
Limitation in Campus: Built for large agrifood supply chains, not daily student use
EXISTING APPROACH 03

ML-based Food Safety Research

Main Contribution: Predicts & assesses contamination risk
Limitation in Campus: Focuses on large-scale data, not campus-level complaints
EXISTING APPROACH 04

Digital Food Inspection Systems

Main Contribution: Supports monitoring & data collection
Limitation in Campus: Doesn't connect student complaints to inspection workflows
