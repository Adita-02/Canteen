Four-Stage Verification Flow

How an automated student complaint evolves into a rigorous laboratory-verified inspection and resolution.

1

AI Risk Flagging

AI flags an item after repeated student complaints (e.g. 5+ hygiene reports in a week or anomaly in sentiment).

2

Department Notification

Pharmacy Department is notified and schedules a surprise or urgent on-site inspection within 60 minutes.

3

Laboratory Audit

Inspector collects samples, checks kitchen temperatures and sanitized surfaces, and files a standardized lab culture report.

4

Verdict & Resolution

Verdict updates the item's public status (Passed / Under Review / Failed) and formally resolves the original complaint.
